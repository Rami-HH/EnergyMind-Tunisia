# Filtered dataset notice

This folder is a **filtered subset** of:

*Industrial Asset-Level Electrical Energy Dataset from a Manufacturing Facility (15-Minute Aggregates)*
(c) 2026 Christopher Flynn / Munster Technological University, licensed CC BY 4.0 (see `LICENSE.txt`
and the original `README.md`, both unchanged).

## What was kept
One coherent slice of the factory power line (declared supply relationships in `metadata/EdgeList.csv`):

    mi_a (grid import) -> sb_c (main distribution) -> sb_b (sub-board) -> 8 loads:
    ahu_a, mix_a, mix_b, mix_c, mix_d, mh_a, u_d, u_e

11 of the 43 assets, full year 2025, 15-minute resolution, Parquet format (`data_parquet/`).

## Changes made (CC BY 4.0 requires indicating changes)
- Whole assets were selected; **no measurement value was modified** and no column was removed.
- The CSV copy (`data_csv/`) was left out to save space (the Parquet data is identical in content).
- `metadata/AssetList.csv`, `EdgeList.csv` and `meter_data_quality_log.csv` were filtered to rows that
  involve only the 11 kept assets. `schema_gold_table.csv` and `README_metadata.md` are unchanged.
- The `code/` and `validation/` folders of the original were left out.
- Relationships describe monitoring/supply context, not strict electrical topology (as in the original).

## Not Tunisian grid data
These are measurements from an industrial facility. Do not describe them as Tunisia's national grid.
Anomalies used in the benchmark are synthetic and generated by `src/energymind/synthetic.py`; the only
real faults are the ones documented in `metadata/meter_data_quality_log.csv`.
